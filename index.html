F/01

Daily limit per app

Search any installed app and set a limit in minutes for each one. The counter resets at midnight — every day is a fresh start.

F/02

Automatic blocking

When the limit is reached, the app closes right away and a screen tells you time's up. Need a little more? Earn 5 extra minutes by watching an ad.

F/03

Runs in the background

Restarts on its own when your phone boots, with a step-by-step permissions guide for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, Oppo, Vivo and other brands.

F/04

Transparent permissions

Android's Accessibility service is used only to detect which app is open and apply the limits you set. No personal data is collected or shared through it.
